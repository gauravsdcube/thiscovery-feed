**17 November 2026**

## Highlights

- The space picker lays spaces out in columns when a member can post in many of them.
- The composer opens with no space selected.
- Administrators can leave Feed off the top bar. Members can still open it at `/feed`.

## Requirements

- HumHub 1.18 or later
- PHP 8.2 or later

## Upgrade

Replace the module with this release and open Administration → Thiscovery Feed. **Show Feed in the top bar** is on, so existing menus stay as they are until you turn it off.
