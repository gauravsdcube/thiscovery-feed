# Thiscovery Feed 1.0.0

**17 November 2026**

Copyright (c) 2026 D Cube Consulting. All rights reserved.

First release of Thiscovery Feed, a HumHub module that gives members a single home feed of the spaces they belong to.

## Highlights

- Three-column feed: shortcuts, the post stream, and announcements. On a smaller screen the stream comes first.
- Members post to every space they can use, or to the spaces they select. The post is saved in each of those spaces. The feed shows it once, with the space names on one line.
- Pinned posts appear in the announcements rail instead of being repeated in the stream.
- Administrators choose whether the feed sits beside the Dashboard or replaces it, and which widgets members see by default.
- Each member can show, hide, reorder, or reset those widgets.

## Requirements

- HumHub 1.18 or later
- PHP 8.2 or later

## Install

1. Place the module in `protected/modules/thiscovery-feed`.
2. Enable **Thiscovery Feed** under Administration → Modules.
3. Open Administration → Thiscovery Feed and set the placement and default widgets.

The feed is available at `/feed`.
